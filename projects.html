Cat Of Mari

This is a game which I made in unity and is a game similar to Katamari Damaci. I made it relatively quickly and really loved making it. The added gimick that makes this different than just regular Katamari is the fact that the ball is on a giant planet.

Cookie Clicker

This is a collab project, where I had someone besides myself finally make the art and help with the sound as well. This give the cookie a good feel, and the sound a nice crunch when clicked. I wanted the game to not go infinitely, due to games like this quickly getting stale without a goal. So, I ended up making last only about 30 minuets of clickingm, and around 1000~2000 clicks to be able to finish the game.

Cube Dragger

This is a game which I made in a day and then worked on over the following weeks. It took quite a while to smooth out all of the movement kinks eventually using a torque based motion system rather than just a move to command. This made the motion feel more natural and fun.

Steroids

This is a small space shooter which I made. Like the last game this was in an hour, which meant I had less time to make it and thus didn't have time to polish it as much. Over all the project scope became about good player communication and some minimal difficulty increase.
